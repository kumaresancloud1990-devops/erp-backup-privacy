ERP Backup - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

"ERP Backup" is a private tool used only by the owner of this business's ERP system to store encrypted copies of the ERP's own backups in the owner's Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

It uses only the Google Drive permission drive.file: it can create files and folders in the signed-in Google Drive and see only the files it created itself (a folder named "ERP Backups"). It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Google account. It also reads the signed-in account's e-mail address, only to show which account is connected.

What it stores in Google Drive

Backup files of the ERP, encrypted on the ERP's own computer before upload, so Google and anyone else without the owner's backup password cannot read them, plus a short manifest listing the backup's file names, sizes and checksums.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or sent to anyone other than Google Drive in the owner's own account. No advertising, analytics or tracking is used.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect at any time in the ERP, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in Google Drive stay there until the owner deletes them.
